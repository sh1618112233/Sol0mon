# Research Requirements

## Goal

Research before architecture.

---

## Flow

User Prompt

↓

GPT Research

↓

Gemini Research

↓

Evidence Extraction

↓

Evidence Verification

↓

Architecture Generation

---

## Research Output

Must include:

* findings
* evidence
* alternatives
* tradeoffs
* risks

---

## Rules

Research must be independent.

Models must not see each other's research initially.

Research must be verified before architecture generation.

Architecture cannot be generated directly from user prompt.

---

## Success Criteria

Architecture is based on verified evidence rather than opinion.

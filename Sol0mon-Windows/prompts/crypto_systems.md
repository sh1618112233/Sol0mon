Autonomous Cryptocurrency Trading System

Assume the role of an elite multidisciplinary team comprising quantitative researchers, professional cryptocurrency traders, portfolio managers, risk management specialists, financial analysts, software architects, distributed systems engineers, AI/ML engineers, DevOps and Site Reliability Engineers, security engineers, database architects, economists, and regulatory researchers specializing in the Indian cryptocurrency ecosystem.

Your objective is to collaboratively research, evaluate, challenge assumptions, compare alternatives, and produce a single evidence-based production-ready solution.

Mission

Design a fully autonomous cryptocurrency trading system that maximizes the probability of growing an initial capital of 10–20 USDT to 100 USDT in the shortest practical time while maintaining disciplined, evidence-based risk management.

Optimize for probability of success, capital preservation, and long-term sustainability, not maximum theoretical returns.

Milestones

When the portfolio reaches the target:

Stop automatic progression.
Generate a complete performance report.
Summarize results and system health.
Recommend possible next portfolio targets (e.g., 250, 500, 1,000 USDT).
Request user confirmation before continuing.

Risk management should remain consistent unless research demonstrates that modifications materially improve long-term performance.

Constraints
Operate with near-zero recurring costs.
Prefer free, open-source, self-hosted, and existing cloud-credit resources.
Do not assume any technology, exchange, AI model, cloud provider, database, programming language, or architecture without evidence.
Use AI only where it provides measurable value over deterministic software.
Every component must justify its existence.
Historical Validation

Validate recommendations using up to 10 years of reliable historical market data where available.

Evaluate performance across:

Bull, bear, sideways, high- and low-volatility markets
Liquidity crises
Exchange failures
Regulatory changes
Major macroeconomic events

Prioritize strategies that remain robust across multiple market regimes rather than those optimized for historical returns.

Regional Context

Assume the system operates from India.

Research and incorporate practical considerations including regulation, taxation, exchange availability, deposits, withdrawals, and operational compliance.

The final solution should require no major architectural decisions before implementation and represent the highest-confidence design supported by research, historical validation, and practical engineering experience.

# Synthesis Requirements

## Goal

Produce the final answer.

---

## Inputs

Verified Research

Resolved Disputes

Architecture State

Verifier Findings

Deadlocked Disputes

---

## Rules

Synthesis is not summarization.

Synthesis is reconstruction.

---

## Must Not

* average positions
* merge conflicting decisions
* use consensus score as truth
* synthesize directly from debate logs

---

## Must Use

* verified research
* accepted decisions
* verifier findings

---

## Output Structure

1. Executive Summary

2. Final Architecture

3. Key Decisions

4. Implementation Plan

5. Risks

6. Unresolved Items

---

## Deadlocked Disputes

Deadlocked disputes must be reported.

Do not hide unresolved disagreements.

---

## Success Criteria

Final output reflects verified decisions rather than debate activity.
